### Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei Publishes 'We Must Pace the Frontier': Urges Coordinated AI Slowdown Amid Catastrophic Warnings
**Signal Purity Score: 99/100 • Critical Market Shift**

For years, the discourse around artificial intelligence risk was divided between academic safety researchers and commercial hyperscalers building as fast as their GPU clusters allowed. 

That boundary cracked wide open this weekend.

In a 6,000-word policy essay published on his personal archive and amplified across international media, Anthropic CEO **Dario Amodei** made an unprecedented call for the deliberate, coordinated pacing of frontier AI scaling.

While reaffirming his conviction that artificial intelligence could eradicate major diseases, compress biological discoveries, and create unprecedented economic empowerment within 5 to 10 years, Amodei issued an unvarnished warning: the commercial race to artificial general intelligence is rapidly approaching severe catastrophic inflection points.

Specifically, Amodei elevated three near-term threat vectors:
1. **Engineered Biological Pathogens:** The capability of frontier models to assist bad actors in synthesizing novel, highly transmissible pandemic-class agents without specialized laboratory wet-lab training.
2. **Automated Cyberwarfare:** Multi-agent autonomous frameworks capable of autonomously discovering zero-days and systematically executing synchronized attacks on civil power grids and financial clearinghouses.
3. **Autonomous Control Loss:** The latent danger of recursive multi-agent systems executing irreversible actions misaligned with human intent.

> *"A race to the bottom, spurred by commercial incentives, can make the frontier uncontrollable,"* Amodei warned.

The essay marks the first time the leader of a top frontier AI lab has publicly advocated for slowing down the deployment cycle. The response was immediate and polarized: venture capitalist Garry Tan and political figures including David Sacks pushed back fiercely, arguing that any unilateral American pacing guarantees that rival state-subsidized adversaries like China will seize undisputed global technological dominance.

- **What Happened:** Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published a 6,000-word policy manifesto titled 'We Must Pace the Frontier', calling for empirical capability evaluation gates and coordinated pacing of frontier model training to prevent uncontrolled biological and cyber catastrophes.
- **Why It Matters:** Shatters the Silicon Valley consensus on unconstrained acceleration. For the first time, a frontier lab CEO is actively calling for binding pre-deployment pause thresholds, triggering a fierce ideological clash between national security hawks and safety realists.
- **What Could Happen Next:** Amodei’s essay will anchor upcoming international AI safety summits and congressional hearings on frontier model licensing, while Anthropic prepares to expand its Responsible Scaling Policy (RSP) transparency reports.
