## 🔴 LEAD INTELLIGENCE REPORT
### Moonshot AI Unveils Kimi K3.1 Pro: 3T-Class Open-Weight Model Scores 65.7% on Terminal-Bench
**Signal Purity Score: 94/100 • Critical Signal**

In a major technical disclosure published on October 23, 2026, Beijing-based artificial intelligence lab Moonshot AI announced Kimi K3.1 Pro, positioning the flagship 3-trillion-parameter-class model as its most capable open-weight architecture to date. Serving as a post-trained successor to Kimi K3, the model combines a 1-million-token context window with comprehensive multimodal reasoning across text, high-resolution vision, and full-frame video. Alongside the primary checkpoint, Moonshot debuted Kimi K3.1 Pro Highspeed, which delivers two to three times faster generation throughput for responsive terminal coding across Kimi.ai, Kimi Work, and the Kimi API.

The architectural gains achieved by K3.1 Pro stem from an overhaul of Moonshot's reinforcement learning infrastructure. Rather than solely scaling pre-training data volume, the laboratory re-engineered its entire post-training execution stack—spanning distributed RL rollouts, KV cache paging, and automated sandbox validation—to coordinate joint reinforcement learning across hundreds of downstream domains. On Terminal-Bench 4.0, which evaluates autonomous coding agents on realistic, resource-constrained terminal tasks under rigid verification tests, K3.1 Pro scored 65.7%, surpassing OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra (58.2%) and closing within a single percentage point of Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5 (66.4%). On Artificial Analysis, K3.1 Pro achieved an Intelligence Index of 55.1, establishing an open-weight position directly on the price-performance Pareto line.

Beyond software development, K3.1 Pro demonstrated significant capabilities in spatial geometry and parametric engineering. On BenchCAD, an evaluation measuring 3D reconstruction from multi-view sketches into executable CAD scripts, K3.1 Pro scored 92.7% when equipped with tool-use connectors, approaching GPT-6 Astra's 95.9%. In practical demonstrations, the model generated interactive Three.js scenes, procedural Godot game mechanics, and designed mechanical components directly in Blender without requiring external MCP bridge middleware. On financial analytics, the model posted 64.4 on OfficeQA Pro v2, cutting factual calculation errors by more than half relative to baseline K3.

Addressing safety governance, Moonshot AI detailed an explicit defensive policy governing offensive cyber capabilities. During development, researchers observed that scaling post-training compute naturally elevated exploit discovery proficiency on software like the Linux kernel and JavaScriptCore. In response, Moonshot systematically identified and purged offensive cybersecurity data from both mid-training and RL environments. On xdev-bench-hard, an unmitigated research checkpoint solved 90.5% of exploit scenarios at pass@3, whereas the shipping K3.1 Pro was constrained to 81.0%, keeping its offensive threshold strictly below frontier proprietary systems while leaving open weights accessible for defensive auditing on November 4.

- **What Happened:** Moonshot AI announced Kimi K3.1 Pro, a 3-trillion-parameter-class open-weight multimodal reasoning model with a 1M context window, outscoring GPT-6 Astra on Terminal-Bench 4.0 at 65.7% and entering Artificial Analysis at index 55.1.
- **Why It Matters:** The release proves that open-weight post-training compute can match proprietary frontier models across long-horizon terminal coding, 3D parametric CAD modeling, and multi-step financial analytics without compromising defensive safety.
- **What Could Happen Next:** K3.1 Pro and its low-latency counterpart K3.1 Pro Highspeed are live today on Kimi.ai and API endpoints, with full public model weights scheduled for open release on November 4, 2026.

### 1. Block Debuts Dessa: Full-Duplex Speech Model Trained Solely on Human Audio
- **Category:** AI / Speech Intelligence & Full-Duplex Agency • **Read:** 5 min • **Signal:** 93/100
- **The Core Signal:** Jack Dorsey's Block Research Lab unveiled Dessa, the world's first conversational model trained exclusively on raw human sound without written text, phonetic transcriptions, or pre-trained LLM foundations. Operating in true full-duplex mode, Dessa listens and speaks concurrently, mastering conversational backchanneling ("yeah", "got it", "ooh"), spontaneous mid-sentence laughter, and emotional prosodic empathy. Remarkably, Block trained the model on a single NVIDIA GB200 NVL72 rack, with local inference executing faster than real time on consumer Apple Silicon (MacBook Pro M4 Max).
- **Bottom Line:** Bypasses the fragile speech-to-text-to-speech cascade, establishing an entirely audio-native paradigm where emotional nuance and human conversational cadence are preserved intact.
