### Anthropic Prices Record $81.3B Nasdaq IPO Ahead of ANT Trading Debut at ~$1.5T Valuation
**Signal Rating: 97/100 | Category: AI Capital Markets & Industry Milestones | Primary Source: Anthropic & BBC News**  
*Read full analysis: https://nextvector.rhasan.online/article/anthropic-prices-record-81b-nasdaq-ipo-ant*

On Wednesday, November 18, 2026, Anthropic, PBC priced its initial public offering at **$83.00 per Class A share**—above its **$70–$78** marketed band—covering **980,000,000 shares** (**956,351,354** primary shares sold by Anthropic and **23,648,646** by selling stockholders) ahead of Thursday's listing on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under ticker **`ANT`**. Surpassing SpaceX's **$75 billion** June 2026 flotation at an implied **~$1.5 trillion valuation**, the listing makes the five-year-old developer behind Claude the first pure-play frontier AI company on U.S. public equities, overtaking Walmart and Samsung in market value while approaching Tesla and Meta.

Key structural and financial figures from Anthropic's pricing notice and BBC reporting:

1. **Up to $107B Total Capital Pool:** Alongside the **>$81 billion (£62 billion)** public offering coordinated with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley, Anthropic can draw **$26 billion** in separate strategic investments from Google, Microsoft, and Nvidia, plus roughly **$12 billion** via a 30-day underwriter option for up to **147,000,000** additional shares.
2. **Scale vs. Cash Burn:** Order books were reportedly **10x oversubscribed** across both institutional and retail buyers, even as prospectus disclosures cited by BBC News showed **$33 billion** in revenue against **$104 billion** in net losses across the nine months through September 2026.
3. **Why List Now:** Empower head of strategy Marta Norton noted that *"the public markets open up the financial spigot a little bit more and so the time has come,"* while Counterpoint Research analyst Yang Wang warned investors will focus on Anthropic's capacity to fund hundreds of billions in approaching obligations ahead of OpenAI's expected 2027 listing.

### 4. Ruby2ßhell Flaw Exposes Default Rails, GitLab, and GitHub Enterprise Server to Unauthenticated RCE
**Signal Rating: 92/100 | Category: Cybersecurity & Runtimes | Primary Source: Veria Labs**  
*Read full report: https://nextvector.rhasan.online/article/ruby2shell-ghsa-34m4-m9fh-gqp5-rails-gitlab-rce*  
Disclosed under **`GHSA-34m4-m9fh-gqp5`** (updated November 19 at 15:29 UTC), **Ruby2ßhell** abuses an unchecked second-byte write when uppercasing **`ß`** -> **`SS`** in legacy single-byte encodings (`ISO-8859`, `Windows-125x`), letting **21 `ß` bytes** overflow Ruby's `input + 20` heap buffer by one controlled byte (`0x53` / `0x73`). Reachable pre-auth in default **Rails** and classic **Sinatra** apps across Ruby `2.4.0–4.0.9`—including current builds of **GitLab 19.6.0**, **GHES 3.22.5**, and **Chatwoot 4.20.0**—operators must upgrade to Ruby **3.3.14**, **3.4.13**, or **4.0.10** (`ruby/ruby@d582f8f`) ahead of PoC publication in one week.
