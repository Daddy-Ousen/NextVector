### The Problem With Modern Tech Media
Most technology coverage today is broken. 

It is dominated by recycled corporate press releases, superficial LinkedIn summaries, sensationalized AI existential dread, and clickbait speculation. Hardware specs are reported without architectural context. Benchmark claims are accepted without validation. Scientific discoveries are reduced to misleading soundbites.

If you are an engineer, researcher, hardware architect, founder, or technical decision-maker, your time is your most constrained asset. You cannot afford to wade through hundreds of noise-filled articles just to discover what actually changed.

**NextVector was created to solve this problem.**

### What We Do
Every single day, we monitor thousands of peer-reviewed papers (arXiv, Nature, Science, IEEE), semiconductor foundry tape-outs, high-performance computing clusters, and foundation model weights. 

We filter out 99% of the noise to bring you the **definitive daily signal**—explained through first-principles engineering and our **Three-Question Framework**:

1. **What Happened?** The exact technical facts, mechanism, and empirical delta.
2. **Why It Matters?** The architectural, economic, and strategic implications for the industry.
3. **What’s Next?** Real-world roadmaps, bottleneck shifts, and upcoming inflection points.

### Our Editorial Covenant
We operate under strict, non-negotiable editorial principles:

- **Primary Source Verification:** We link directly to original papers, open-source repositories, and validated patent filings. No secondary aggregators.
- **Zero Sponsored Editorial:** We never accept payment to cover a model, company, or hardware vendor. Our analysis is independent and uncompromising.
- **Architectural Depth Over Hype:** If a benchmark is contaminated or a performance claim is overstated, we call it out with data.
- **Respect for Your Time:** We write with high information density. No filler sentences, no algorithmic SEO fluff.
